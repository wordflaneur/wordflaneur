🔒 Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Here's how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Last updated: March 15, 2026

📋 1. Introduction

Welcome to WordFlaneur. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

By using WordFlaneur, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We use your data to provide and improve our services, and we never sell your personal information to third parties.

📊 2. What Data We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Data: Name, email address, username, password (hashed), and profile information.
  • Contact Information: Email address for account verification, notifications, and support.
  • Payment Information: Payment details are processed by our payment partners. We do not store full credit card numbers.

Activity Data

  • Reading History: What stories you read, chapters you unlock, and your reading progress.
  • Interaction Data: Comments, reviews, votes, hearts, and recommendations you make.
  • Social Data: Which scouts you follow and who follows you.
  • Earnings Data: Transaction history, earnings, and payment records (for authors and scouts).

Technical Data

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, and interactions with the platform.
  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device to improve your experience.

⚙️ 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • Providing Services: To operate and maintain the platform, deliver content, and process transactions.
  • Improving Experience: To analyze usage patterns and improve our platform, features, and user experience.
  • Communication: To send notifications, updates, and promotional content (you can opt out).
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Earnings & Payments: To calculate and process earnings for authors and scouts.
  • Personalization: To recommend stories, scouts, and content tailored to your interests.

We never sell your personal data to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

🔄 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share your data only when necessary:

  • Service Providers: We use third-party services for payment processing, hosting, email delivery, and analytics. These providers have limited access to your data.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose data if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the platform.
  • Public Profiles: Your public profile (username, display name, bio, and stories) is visible to all users. You control what you share.
  • Scouts and Authors: Scouts see the stories they recommend. Authors see readers who unlock their content.

We do not share your private data with advertisers or marketing companies.

🔐 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest.
  • Access Control: Strict access controls limit who can access user data.
  • Regular Audits: We conduct regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • Secure Payments: All payment transactions are processed through PCI-DSS compliant partners.

While we strive to protect your data, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication when available.

🍪 6. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience on WordFlaneur. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for platform functionality (login, session management, preferences).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with the platform.
  • Preference Cookies: Store your preferences (theme, language, reading settings).

You can manage your cookie preferences in your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect platform functionality.

👤 7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction: You can update or correct your data at any time in your profile settings.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Data Portability: You can request your data in a portable format.
  • Opt-Out: You can opt out of marketing communications and non-essential cookies.

To exercise your rights, please contact us at privacy@wordflaneur.com or use the settings in your dashboard.

🧒 8. Children's Privacy

WordFlaneur is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

🌍 9. International Data Transfers

WordFlaneur operates globally. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your jurisdiction. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with applicable laws.

By using WordFlaneur, you consent to the transfer of your data to countries where we operate.

📝 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through:

  • In-platform notifications
  • Email notifications
  • Banners on the platform

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

📧 11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, our data practices, or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us:
