About WordFlaneur

A sanctuary for slow-burn stories. Where authors write at their own pace, readers discover hidden gems, and scouts earn from recommendations they genuinely love.

📖 What is WordFlaneur?

WordFlaneur is a platform for slow-burn storytelling. Unlike platforms that pressure authors to publish daily, we believe that great stories take time — and that readers appreciate the craft.

We're built around three core principles:

  • Quality over Speed — Write at your own pace. No daily pressure.
  • Readers Are Curators — Scouts discover and promote stories they genuinely love.
  • Everyone Can Earn — Authors earn from readers. Scouts earn from recommendations. The platform takes a small cut to grow.

👥 Who Belongs Here?

WordFlaneur has three primary roles. You can be one, two, or all three.

⚙️ How It Works

For Authors

  • Publish your story chapter by chapter. There's no minimum publishing frequency.
  • Lock chapters behind a paywall — readers pay to unlock. You keep 80% of the revenue.
  • Receive tips from readers. You keep 90% of tips.
  • Readers can subscribe to you. You keep 80% of subscription revenue.
  • Scouts will discover and recommend your work — if it's good.
  • Your earnings are fully transparent in your dashboard.

For Scouts

  • Discover stories you genuinely love. Only recommend what you believe in.
  • Write a personal review explaining why you recommend each story.
  • Build a following of readers who trust your taste.
  • Earn tips from readers who appreciate your recommendations.
  • Earn cumulative rewards from the platform based on your Trust Value.
  • Your Trust Value is a metric that measures sincerity, consistency, and reader engagement.
  • Higher Trust Value = more visibility = more followers = more rewards.

For Readers

  • Read stories for free (first few chapters) or unlock everything with a Premium subscription.
  • Follow scouts to discover great stories through people you trust.
  • Build your public library — show what you're reading and support the stories you love.
  • Tip scouts and authors directly.
  • Subscribe to your favorite authors for early access and exclusive content.

🔒 Trust Value

Trust Value is the single most important metric for scouts. It measures how much readers trust your recommendations.

Trust Value is built through:

  • Sincerity — Recommending stories you genuinely love.
  • Consistency — Regular recommendations over time.
  • Engagement — Hearts, votes, and comments on your recommendations.
  • Reader feedback — How readers respond to your recommendations.

Scouts with higher Trust Value get more visibility — they appear in banners, rankings, and recommendations more often.

"We don't reveal the exact formula — that's what keeps it honest. But we reward sincerity, not gaming the system."

🔍 Transparency

We believe in different transparency for different roles. Here's what you can expect:

  • Authors — Full transparency on earnings. You'll see exactly what you earn, from every source, every time.
  • Scouts — Partial transparency on rewards. You'll see your total rewards and Trust Value growth, but not the exact formula.
  • Readers — Minimal transparency. You'll see story quality, scout reputation, and clear pricing.

📋 Rules & Guidelines

For Authors

  • You own your work. WordFlaneur does not claim ownership of your stories.
  • You can lock chapters for profit — or keep everything free.
  • You can route readers to Patreon or other platforms, but you'll lose platform visibility.
  • Your earnings are fully transparent in your dashboard.

For Scouts

  • Only recommend stories you genuinely love. Readers trust you because you're authentic.
  • Your Trust Value is built on sincerity, consistency, and engagement.
  • You can earn tips from readers and rewards from the platform.
  • Scouts who game the system (e.g., recommending for money) will lose Trust Value.

For Everyone

  • Be kind. WordFlaneur is a community of readers, writers, and curators.
  • No spam, no harassment, no plagiarism.
  • Content warnings are required for mature content.
  • AI disclosure is required — be transparent about how you create.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I own my work?

Yes. You own everything you publish on WordFlaneur. We take no ownership.

Can I leave the platform?

Yes. You can take your work down at any time. You can also route readers to Patreon or other platforms, but you'll lose platform visibility.

How do scouts earn?

Scouts earn from reader tips and cumulative platform rewards. The more sincere and trusted you are, the more you earn.

What is Trust Value?

Trust Value is a metric that measures how much readers trust a scout's recommendations. It's built through sincerity, consistency, and engagement.

How do I get paid?

Earnings are tracked in coins. You can convert coins to real money via our payment partner (coming soon).
