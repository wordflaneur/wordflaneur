✍️ For Authors

How do I publish a story? ▼
Go to your Dashboard and click "New Story". Fill in the title, description, genre, tags, and upload a cover image. You can then add chapters one by one. Stories are published immediately unless you save them as drafts.
How do I earn money? ▼
You can earn through three main channels: Chapter Unlocks (readers pay to unlock chapters, you keep 80%), Reader Tips (you keep 90%), and Subscriptions (you keep 80%). All earnings are tracked in your dashboard in real-time.
Do I need to publish daily? ▼
No! WordFlaneur is built for slow-burn storytelling. There is no minimum publishing frequency. You publish when you're ready. We encourage regular updates (e.g., once a week) to build trust with your readers, but you set your own pace.
Who owns my stories? ▼
You do. WordFlaneur does not claim ownership of your stories. You retain all rights. By publishing, you grant us a non-exclusive license to host and distribute your content on the platform.
How do I get paid? ▼
Earnings are tracked in coins. You can convert coins to real money via our payment partners. The minimum payout threshold is $10. Payouts are processed monthly. Check your Dashboard for details.

🔦 For Scouts

How do I become a Scout? ▼
Anyone can become a Scout! Simply start recommending stories you love. Write a personal review explaining why you recommend each story. Build a following of readers who trust your taste. You'll start earning as readers tip you and as your Trust Value grows.
What is Trust Value? ▼
Trust Value is a metric that measures how much readers trust your recommendations. It's built through sincerity (recommending stories you genuinely love), consistency (regular recommendations), and engagement (hearts, votes, comments on your recommendations). Higher Trust Value = more visibility = more followers = more rewards.
How do Scouts earn money? ▼
Scouts earn through two channels: Reader Tips (you keep 90%) and Platform Rewards (based on your Trust Value and platform contributions). You earn 100% of platform rewards (no fee).
Can I recommend my own stories? ▼
No. Scouts should recommend stories they genuinely love. Recommending your own work undermines trust and will negatively affect your Trust Value. If you're an author, focus on writing great stories — Scouts will find you.

📚 For Readers

How do I discover new stories? ▼
Follow Scouts whose taste you trust. Browse the Rankings for trending, popular, and top-rated stories. Use the Browse page to filter by genre, trope, and tags.
Can I read stories for free? ▼
Yes! Many stories offer free chapters. Authors decide how many chapters to make free and which to lock. You can read free chapters without any cost. To unlock locked chapters, you can pay per chapter or subscribe to the author.
What is a Reader Subscription? ▼
The Reader Subscription is a premium membership ($4.99/mo) that gives you unlimited reading of locked chapters, early access to new chapters, and exclusive content. You also support the platform and the authors you love.
How do I follow a Scout? ▼
Go to a Scout's profile and click the "Follow" button. You'll see their recommendations in your feed. Following Scouts helps you discover great stories through people you trust.

👤 Account & Billing

How do I delete my account? ▼
Go to Settings → Account → "Delete Account". Follow the confirmation prompts. Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data from WordFlaneur. This action cannot be undone.
How do I update my billing information? ▼
Go to Settings → Billing. You can update your payment method, view your subscription status, and manage your earnings payouts here.
Is my data secure? ▼
Yes. We use industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption, secure payment processing, and regular security audits. We never sell your personal data to third parties. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.

🛍️ Marketplace

What is the Marketplace? ▼
The Marketplace is a directory of vetted service providers including editors, cover artists, translators, and more. Authors can find professionals to help with their stories. All providers are vetted by the WordFlaneur team.
How do I list my services? ▼
If you're a service provider (editor, cover artist, translator, etc.), you can apply to list your services in the Marketplace. Go to Dashboard → Marketplace → "Apply as a Provider". You'll need to submit your portfolio and credentials for review.
What fees apply to Marketplace transactions? ▼
Service providers keep 85% of transaction revenue. The platform takes a 15% fee to cover vetting, hosting, and transaction processing. All fees are transparently shown before you confirm a transaction.
